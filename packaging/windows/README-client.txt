GETTING STARTED

  1. Open the app from the "Veridian E-invoicing Pakistan" icon on the
     desktop or in the Start menu. It opens in a window of its own.
  2. The first time, a short setup wizard asks for your business details
     exactly as registered on IRIS.
  3. You start in the training simulator: nothing is sent to FBR until you
     connect the company to FBR (Settings > FBR integration).

  The User guide (Start menu, or docs\Veridian-E-invoicing-Pakistan-User-Guide.pdf)
  explains every screen.


WHERE THINGS ARE

  Program   this folder
  Data      C:\ProgramData\VeridianEInvoicingPakistan
            the database, settings, certificates, logs and backups.
            Keep it safe: sales tax records must be kept for six years.
            Uninstalling the program never deletes it.
  Guides    docs\  (user guide, installation, FBR onboarding and
            compliance, mobile access, ERP integration API)


OTHER COMPUTERS AND PHONES

  In the app, open Support > Mobile app & access for the address, a QR code
  and the certificate that other devices need.
